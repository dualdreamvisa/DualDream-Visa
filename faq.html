Frequently Asked Questions

Do you guarantee visa approval?

We provide expert guidance and complete documentation support, which significantly increases chances of approval. However, final decision rests with immigration authorities.

How long does the process take?

Tourist visas may take 2‑4 weeks; work permits can vary from 2‑8 months depending on category and country.

Is the counselling really free?

Yes, the initial assessment is completely free. We only charge after you decide to proceed with our services.

Can I apply from any country?

Absolutely. We assist applicants worldwide through our online platform.
